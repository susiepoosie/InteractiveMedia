Act_01: Speculative Objects

We were given a 8x8 grid on graph paper, on which we brainstormed potential ideas for our final project. On the top, we listed 8 inputs, and on the left, 8 outputs. For each square, I wrote down the first concept I could think of, linking that particular input and output together. This left us with ideas for various sensory devices, which we then discussed with peers to further develop the most interesting ones.
